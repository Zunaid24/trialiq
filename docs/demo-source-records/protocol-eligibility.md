# Protocol Synopsis & Eligibility Criteria

**Study:** Phase II Study of XYZ-101 in Advanced Non-Small Cell Lung Cancer  
**Sponsor:** TrialIQ Biopharma

## Inclusion criteria
1. Age ≥18 years.
2. Histologically confirmed NSCLC.
3. Stage III or IV disease.
4. ECOG Performance Status 0–1.
5. Measurable disease per RECIST 1.1.
6. Life expectancy ≥12 weeks.
7. ALT ≤2 × ULN.
8. AST ≤2 × ULN.
9. Bilirubin ≤1.5 × ULN.
10. Hemoglobin ≥9 g/dL.
11. Platelets ≥100,000/mm³.
12. ANC ≥1,500/mm³.
13. Signed informed consent.

## Key exclusion criteria
- Active brain metastases.
- Prior treatment with XYZ-101.
- Pregnancy.
- Active hepatitis B or C.
- Severe uncontrolled infection.
- Autoimmune disease requiring systemic treatment.
- Recent major surgery.
- ECOG >1.

## Screening note
MRI is required to confirm measurable disease and evaluate active brain metastases. Missing documentation is pending rather than automatically negative.
