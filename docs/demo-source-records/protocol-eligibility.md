## Key exclusion criteria

- Active brain metastases.
- Prior treatment with XYZ-101.
- Pregnancy.
- Active hepatitis B or C.
- Severe uncontrolled infection.
- Autoimmune disease requiring systemic treatment.
- Recent major surgery.
- ECOG >1.
