# TrialIQ AI quality standard

TrialIQ AI output is evaluated for evidence coverage, specificity, actionability, uncertainty, contradictions, and decision readiness.

## Required output chain

**Observed evidence → Clinical interpretation → Why it matters → Risk/priority → Evidence gaps → Review action**

The model must distinguish observed facts from interpretation and must not present an unsupported clinical conclusion as fact.

## Product principle

TrialIQ is not intended to be another clinical summarizer. Its value is reducing review effort by connecting evidence, reasoning, uncertainty, and the next review action.
