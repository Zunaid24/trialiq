# Clinical reviewer checklist

Before accepting an AI finding, verify:

- The cited source evidence actually supports the statement.
- Interpretation is clearly separated from observed fact.
- The explanation answers why the finding matters.
- Priority reflects review urgency rather than an unsupported diagnosis.
- Missing evidence is explicit.
- The proposed action is concrete and reviewable.
- Contradictory records are surfaced rather than silently reconciled.
- Decision readiness is explained.
